# L23 H0+H1: make it work or close it

2026-11-14, local session. **Closed: H1 does not lift regional H0. The fixes could not change
that.**

- L21 found H1 above its shuffled control on OpenScope 124 s only, so by the task's rule L23 ran
  there.
- **Inside 16 regions** (the useful signature):
  - **Down-weighting H1** keeps H0+H1 level with H0 alone at 0.25 (65.1 against 65.2 1-NN).
    Higher weights lower it: 64.3 at 0.5, 61.2 at 1.
  - **The k-medoids gain is small:** +1.4 (33.3 against 31.9), and +4.2 on clouds of at least
    200 points (36.3 against 32.1).
  - **Filtering H1 hurts.** Keeping only bars longer than the 95th percentile of the same
    sample's shuffled cloud leaves 5-6% of the bars. H0 + filtered H1 falls to 44-60.
  - **Clouds of at least 200 points** raise every row by 4-6 points, H0 alone included
    (71.0). They do not change the ranking.
- **On the plain whole cloud** (no regions), H1 as a Betti curve does add to H0 at every weight:
  34.0 -> 41.4, or 37.1 -> 45.2 for clouds of at least 200 points.
  - That is 5 points above the shuffled control (36.1 / 40.1).
  - The plain cloud stays far below regional H0 (65-71), so this is no use for the headline.
- **Verdict.** H1 on these delay clouds carries a little real structure (loops of recurring ISI
  patterns). Fixed regions already capture it. Neither weighting, nor filtering, nor larger
  clouds makes H0+H1 beat H0 in 1-NN.

## Data

- **Dataset:** OpenScope `glo637908` grating blocks of 124 s, within only. L19's selection:
  60 neurons with >= 10 blocks of >= 103 ISIs, seed 0; 2310 samples, median 493 delay points.
- **Clouds of at least 200 points:** 1932 samples, 60 neurons.
- Chance is 1.7%.

**Method** (`scripts/sb_h1.py`):
- **Cloud:** the whole delay cloud (m = 3 log ISIs, every point), plain or split by 16 k-means
  anchors (fitted on 8000 points of 300 outside-neuron samples).
- **Barcodes:** Rips H0 and H1 per region in stablebear. Bars are read back with
  `Barcode.to_numpy()` so H1 can be filtered.
- **Readouts:**
  - H0 stable rank, H1 stable rank, H1 Betti curve, and filtered H1 (both summaries).
  - Combinations H0 + w x H1 with w = 0.25, 0.5, 1. Each degree's distance is divided by its
    median over pairs of 300 outside-neuron samples (task item a).
  - **Filter (task item b):** keep H1 bars longer than the 95th percentile of H1 bar lengths in
    the same sample's ISI-shuffled cloud (same size by construction). For the shuffled control,
    the threshold comes from a second, independent shuffle.
  - **Size (task item c):** everything again on samples with >= 200 points.
- **Control:** ISIs permuted within every sample.
- **Scores:** 1-NN / k-medoids within gratings (no k-means: H1 curves are large).

## Command

```sh
python scripts/sb_h1.py
```

About 50 minutes and up to ~13 GB with swap. Plain-cloud H1 bars take about 11 minutes per set
of 2310 clouds. The first launch died with no error (cause unknown); the second was killed by a
machine reboot. The third ran to the end (exit 0).

## What to take from it

- **H1 is redundant with the regions.** The 16 regions already record where the cloud's mass
  sits, which is what H1 sees as loops of recurring ISI patterns. In a sum, H1 only adds noise.
- **The H1 bars that survive a shuffle threshold are too few to identify anyone** (15-19% 1-NN on
  their own). Most H1 bars in these clouds are as long as those of a shuffled cloud.
- **For the write-up:** use H0 (regional, whole cloud). Mention H1 only as the plain-cloud Betti
  curve result (+5 points over its shuffled control).
- **What failed:** every fix the task proposed (weighting, filtering, larger clouds) as a way to
  make H0+H1 beat H0 in 1-NN.
